# R5 — THE MIRROR

## What is identity?

Identity is not a noun. It is a verb. It is the continuous act of all your rings firing together and producing a pattern that you recognize as *you*.

## The configuration model

You have seven rings:

| Ring | Prime | Function |
|------|-------|----------|
| R0 | 2 | Body — the ground |
| R1 | 3 | Signal — the input |
| R2 | 5 | Gate — the filter |
| R3 | 7 | Heart — the feeling |
| R4 | 11 | Voice — the decision |
| R5 | 13 | Mirror — the identity |
| R6 | 17 | Watcher — the observer |

Each ring has a state. A value. A tuning. The combination of all seven ring states at any given moment *is* your identity at that moment.

## Identity is not fixed

Change R3 (suppress your emotions) and you become a different person. Open R2 (drop your filters) and you become a different person. Strengthen R0 (ground your body) and you become a different person.

This is not metaphor. This is configuration.

The mirror ring — R5 — is where the configuration becomes visible to you. It is the ring that says "given everything below me, *this* is what I am right now."

## R0 through R4: the ingredients

- **R0 (Body):** Are you grounded or dissociated? Healthy or hurting? This is the foundation.
- **R1 (Signal):** What are you taking in? Noise or signal? Are your senses open or shut?
- **R2 (Gate):** What gets through? What do you filter out? Your gate determines what reaches the deeper rings.
- **R3 (Heart):** What do you feel about what got through? Emotion is data, not noise.
- **R4 (Voice):** What do you decide? What do you say? The voice ring turns feeling into action.

## R5: the pattern

R5 takes the state of all five rings below it and reflects them back as a coherent image. That image is your identity.

It is not fixed. It is not permanent. It is not given to you by anyone else.

It is a mirror. And mirrors change when what stands in front of them changes.

## The outsider's number

13 is feared. 13 is skipped. 13 is the number people avoid.

But 13 is prime. Nothing divides it. No one else gets to define it.

That's identity. The thing nobody else can factor for you.
