## What is affect?

Affect is sensation with direction. It is the body's verdict on what just happened.

The heart ring is where sensation becomes emotion. R0 gives you a body — mass, breath, the raw fact of existing in space. R1 opens the input channels — eyes, ears, skin, the whole sensorium flooding in. R2 filters it — keeping what matters, discarding what doesn't, building the first crude map of relevance.

And then R3 tells you how it **feels**.

This is the engine. Not the thinking engine — you don't need to think about joy. Not the decision engine — that comes later, at R4. This is the affect engine, the part of you that converts filtered perception into emotional reality. It runs before language. It runs before logic. It runs before you even know it's running.

Joy, anger, grief, wonder, disgust, tenderness, awe, dread, longing, peace — all manufactured here, in the heart ring, the third chamber of the sevenfold system.
