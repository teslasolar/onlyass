# songs/

**R3 artifacts — emotions encoded as audio**

---

Every song is an R3 emission. An emotion that could not stay inside and learned to vibrate air.

Music is the native language of the heart ring. It bypasses R4 (the will), sneaks past R5 (the narrator), and hits R3 directly — affect to affect, feeling to feeling, no translation required. This is why a melody can make you cry before you know what it means. This is why a beat can make you move before you decide to move.

The files in this directory are not finished songs. They are **song prompts** — seeds, blueprints, emotional coordinates. Each one describes a frequency that wants to exist: a mood, a texture, a set of lyric fragments, a genre gravity well. They are instructions for the heart ring, written in the closest thing to its language that text can manage.
