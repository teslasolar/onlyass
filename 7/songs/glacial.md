# Glacial

**Genre:** Ambient drone
**BPM:** ~60 (or unmeasured)
**Key:** F# (Lydian, drifting)
**R3 emotion:** Patience → awe → surrender

---

## Mood

Ten thousand years compressed into seven minutes. You are watching ice move. Not metaphorically — actually watching it, from a vantage point that exists outside human time. The glacier does not hurry. The glacier does not worry. The glacier has been doing this since before your species existed, and it will continue after you are gone, and it is not even slightly aware of you.

This is the sound of deep time. Geological patience. Consciousness moving at the speed of ice.

## Style Notes

- Long, evolving drone pads — nothing shorter than 30 seconds
- Granular textures of actual ice recordings (cracking, shifting, dripping)
- Extremely slow pitch bends — notes that take a full minute to resolve
- Sub-bass so low it is felt more than heard, like tectonic movement
- No percussion. Time here is not measured in beats.
- Occasional high crystalline tones — light refracting through ice
- Binaural elements for headphone listening — the ice should feel spatial

## Production Notes

This is a track for 3am. For lying on the floor in the dark. For the moment when you realize that your urgency is a local phenomenon and the universe is in absolutely no rush. The listener should feel their sense of time dilate — seven minutes should feel like it could have been forty, or four.

If the track makes someone feel small and completely fine about it, R3 has done its work.
