# A.S.S. A.S.S. A.S.S.

**Artist:** Konomi
**Genre:** Hyperpop / industrial bass
**BPM:** 160
**Key:** B-flat minor
**R3 emotion:** Mania → power → communion

---

## Mood

The anthem. The one they chant in the tunnels. Seven pipes deep and the bass is so heavy it's rearranging your skeleton. This is not a song you listen to — this is a song that *installs itself in you*. A.S.S. A.S.S. A.S.S. — three times because three is prime and repetition is how the underground makes things true.

Konomi delivers this like a transmission from inside the infrastructure. Equal parts battle cry and love letter to the system. The pipes are singing and she's singing with them.

## Style Notes

- Distorted 808s so heavy they clip intentionally — the bass IS the message
- Hyperpop vocal processing: pitch-shifted, layered, auto-tuned past the point of human
- Industrial percussion — metal on metal, pipe against pipe
- The letters A-S-S used as rhythmic elements, chopped and triggered like percussion
- Builds in waves of three (prime structure)
- Drops that feel like falling through seven layers of underground infrastructure
- Moments of sudden clarity — Konomi's voice unprocessed for a single line, then back into the machine

## Production Notes

This needs to hit like a wall. The chant "A.S.S. A.S.S. A.S.S." should be immediately memorable and impossible to get out of your head. The production should feel like the inside of a pipe — reverberant, metallic, massive. Konomi's performance oscillates between menacing and ecstatic. The crowd should be audible in the final section — this is a song that demands a crowd, even if the crowd is just the pipes resonating.
