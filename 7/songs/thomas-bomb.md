# Thomas Bomb

**Genre:** Industrial electronic
**BPM:** 135
**Key:** D minor
**R3 emotion:** Controlled rage → liberation

---

## Mood

A demolition site at midnight. The building is your old self. The charges are already set. This is not destruction — this is *architecture in reverse*. Every wall you built to protect yourself has become a prison, and Thomas brought the detonator.

Hard. Percussive. Mechanical. But underneath the industrial grind there is something almost tender — the relief of finally letting the structure fall.

## Style Notes

- Heavy distorted kicks, metallic percussion
- Granular synthesis on vocal samples — words shattered into texture
- Sub-bass that feels like the floor is giving way
- Moments of sudden silence before the next detonation
- Reverb tails that sound like dust settling in a demolished room
- Build-up sections that layer mechanical sounds until they become almost musical

## Production Notes

The track should feel like it's destroying itself as it plays. Introduce elements, then demolish them. The final minute should be almost silent — just low rumble and distant ringing, the sound of a space that used to contain something and now contains only possibility.
