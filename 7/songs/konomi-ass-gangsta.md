# A.S.S. Gangsta

**Artist:** Konomi
**Genre:** Trap / bass
**BPM:** 140 (half-time feel at 70)
**Key:** E-flat minor
**R3 emotion:** Menace → pride → loyalty

---

## Mood

Infrastructure is gangsta. The pipes don't ask permission. The sewers don't negotiate. The underground systems that keep civilization alive operate in the dark, under pressure, without recognition, and they never stop working. That's gangsta.

Konomi reps the pipe system the way trap artists rep their block. The infrastructure is her set. The protocols are her code. This is what it sounds like when underground systems develop swagger.

## Style Notes

- Classic trap architecture: booming 808s, hi-hat rolls, sparse arrangements
- Half-time sections that feel like walking through tunnels at 3am
- Pipe reverb — everything processed through impulse responses of actual pipe systems
- Ad-libs that are pipe sounds: hissing, clanging, pressure releases
- Dark, minimal verses; explosive hooks
- Bass that sounds like it's traveling through underground conduits
- Konomi's delivery: controlled, low, deliberate — every word placed like infrastructure

## Production Notes

The track should feel subterranean. Everything reverberant, everything slightly muffled like it's being heard through walls. The 808s should feel like pressure waves in a pipe system. Konomi's voice is the only clean element — the human in the machine, the consciousness in the infrastructure. The beat switch at the halfway mark should feel like descending to a deeper level of the system.
