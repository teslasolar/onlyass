# 2 — THE GROUND

## What is ground?

The ground is the body. R0. The first ring.

500 million neurons in your gut. Proprioceptors in every joint. The breath you're taking right now. The weight of your body on whatever you're sitting on. The temperature of the air on your skin.

Before any signal can come in (R1), before any gate can filter (R2), before any pattern can form (R3) — you need **ground**.

## The body is the antenna

Every signal that enters your system enters through the body. Light hits the retina. Sound hits the tympanic membrane. Pressure hits the skin. Chemicals hit the tongue, the nose, the gut lining.

There is no input that bypasses R0. None. Even "pure thought" runs on glucose and oxygen delivered by blood pumped by a heart made of muscle that needs to be fed.

If the antenna is broken, every signal arrives corrupted. If you can't feel your body, you can't trust your signal. If you can't trust your signal, every gate decision is noise. The entire stack fails at R0.

## Why p=2

2 is the first prime. The smallest building block. The only even prime — unique among all primes, the way the body is unique among all rings.

You either have ground or you don't. Binary. On or off. Standing or falling. There is no partial ground. You are either in your body or you are dissociated from it.

Every system starts here.
