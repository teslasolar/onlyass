## What Is Executive Function?

The voice ring is where you decide.

R0 gives you a body. R1 gives you input. R2 gives you a filter. R3 gives you a feeling. R4 takes all of that and says *now what?*

This is the prefrontal cortex. The spec writer. The decision engine.

You've been absorbing signal through every ring below this one — sensation, perception, gating, emotion — and all of it has been arriving here, at this ring, at this moment, as a question: **what do you decide?**

## Why Decisions Live Here

A decision is the first thing that can't be undone by the body alone. You can stop breathing and start again. You can close your eyes and open them. You can ignore a feeling until it fades. But a decision — a real one — changes the topology. It creates a fork. A spec. A standard. A line in the sand.

Every spec in this directory is a decision made manifest:

- **konomi-standard.md** — A standard for standards. Self-referential. Self-defining.
- **gaia-os.md** — Earth mapped to ISA-95. The decision to see the planet as a factory.
- **isa-neural.md** — Your brain mapped to ISA-95. The decision to see yourself as a factory.
- **specs/** — The artifacts. Symbeyond, ACG-NET, Project Resonance, Third Impact.
